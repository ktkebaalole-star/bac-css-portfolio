About Me

## About Me I am a first-year Bachelor of Science in Financial Technology student at Botswana School of Business Sciences, driven by a strong interest in finance, computer science, and data science. I chose FinTech because it brings these fields together, allowing me to explore how technology and data can be used to understand financial markets, solve problems, and develop innovative financial solutions. My interests extend beyond the classroom. I presented **APEX**, a concept for a market evaluation engine aimed at improving how information is analysed to estimate the value of assets. I am also interested in algorithmic trading, developing quantitative models, exploring proprietary trading, and creating digital payment solutions that could expand monetisation opportunities for creators in Botswana. Although I am still discovering which path I want to pursue, I am committed to building my knowledge, strengthening my technical skills, and exploring ideas that connect finance with technology. I see my academic journey as an opportunity to turn curiosity into practical skills and, eventually, meaningful solutions for Botswana and beyond.
